📐 Reliability-Aware Weight Optimization
SLSQP Solver

Unlike standard stacking which minimizes only clean loss, the research pipeline formulates a constrained bi-objective optimization problem over 5-fold Out-of-Fold (OOF) cross-validation predictions:

minw λclean · L(y, Pclean w) + λadv · L(y, Padv w)
s.t. ∑ wi = 1,   wi ≥ 0

With λclean = 0.5 and λadv = 0.5, the SLSQP solver allocates 78.2% weight to CatBoost due to its superior resistance to gradient leakage in oblivious decision trees, 19.35% to XGBoost, and 2.45% to AdaBoost.
